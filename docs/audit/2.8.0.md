# The 2.8 audit

A full audit of 2.8.0 (`d1e843b`), made on 2026-11-18, before 2.9 was
planned. It read the code, the tests, the docs and CI against nineteen
dimensions, grouped in six areas, and ran a reproducer for most findings.
Its findings decide what 2.9 fixes.

Ids read `A28-<AREA>-<NN>`, and the CHANGELOG cites them as `(audit
A28-REL-01)`. The prefix keeps them apart from the 0.9.0 audit's `PERF-` and
`TEST-` ids, on [the audit ids page](0.9.0.md).

A security finding that is still open is listed by its id and severity only.
It is described here once a release fixes it, as on the audit ids page.

The audit found 61 issues: 0 critical, 10 high, 27 medium, 24 low. Two
more were found in the work for 2.9, A28-SEC-06 (medium) and A28-SEC-07
(low), and are listed with them. Statuses are as of the audit; each is
updated when a release fixes it. The grades are the audit's, of 2.8.0.
